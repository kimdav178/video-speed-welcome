Video Speed Controller

Privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026. This policy covers the Video Speed Controller browser extension and its paid “All Speeds” upgrade. It is published by the developer of the extension, the publisher shown on its Chrome Web Store page (“we”).

Stored on your device only

Your settings (default speed, step, rewind interval, switches), the last speed you set and, if you turn it on, a speed per website. These stay in your browser’s extension storage and are never sent anywhere. The extension does not read, collect or send the content of the pages you visit or your browsing history.

The license check

To know whether all speeds are unlocked, the extension creates a random install ID (not linked to your name, email or browser profile) and sends it, together with the extension version, to our license server at vsc-license.vercel.app (hosted by Vercel). The server sees your IP address as part of the request and uses it only to determine your country, because the paid upgrade is offered in some countries only. The IP address is not stored. The server stores the install ID, whether it has a purchase, and daily per-country counts of checks.

If you buy All Speeds

Payment happens on a checkout page run by Stripe for our payment provider EasyPay. Your card details are handled by Stripe and never reach the extension or our server. After the payment we receive: the install ID, the plan you bought, the subscription status and paid period, the payment ID and your email address. We use the email only to restore your purchase in another browser and to answer your support requests. Stripe’s privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data for anything other than providing the extension and the upgrade.

Our web pages

The welcome, thank-you and uninstall pages on kimdav178.github.io use Umami, a cookie-free analytics service, to count visits. The uninstall page embeds a Google Form that you may fill in voluntarily.

How long we keep data

Purchase records are kept while the purchase is active and for as long as needed to handle refunds, disputes and accounting. You can ask us to delete your email at any time; the purchase then stays tied to the install ID only.

Your rights

You can ask what we store about your install or purchase, ask for a correction, or ask for deletion. Write to us through the contact form: forms.gle/mNnwQanA5D8eQSXXA. Include your install ID (shown on the extension’s upgrade page) or the email from your receipt.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
