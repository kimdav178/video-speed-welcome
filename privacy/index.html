Video Speed Controller

Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026. This policy covers the Video Speed Controller browser extension and its paid “All Speeds” upgrade. It is published by the developer of the extension, the publisher shown on its Chrome Web Store page (“we”), who is responsible for the data described below. Contact: kimdav178@gmail.com.

Stored on your device only

Your settings (default speed, step, rewind interval, switches), the last speed you set and, if you turn it on, a speed per website. During the free trial of all speeds, the extension also keeps a running total of how long you played media at speeds the free version does not have, per speed, so it can show you that total when the trial ends. These stay in your browser’s extension storage and are never sent anywhere. The extension does not read, collect or send the content of the pages you visit or your browsing history.

The license check

To know whether all speeds are unlocked, the extension creates a random install ID (not linked to your name, email or browser profile) and sends it, together with the extension version and the date the extension was installed, to our license server at vsc-license.vercel.app (hosted by Vercel). The server sees your IP address as part of the request and uses it only to determine your country, because the paid upgrade is offered in some countries only. The IP address is not stored. The server stores the install ID if it has a purchase, and daily statistics: per-country counts of checks, and how many different installs checked in per day, per country, per extension version and per install date. These counts are kept with HyperLogLog, a counting method that estimates how many different IDs it has seen without keeping the IDs, so the install ID cannot be read back out of them.

Anonymous step counts

To see where people stop on the way to the upgrade, the extension tells our server, once per install, when it reaches certain steps: it was installed, the speed was changed for the first time, the trial started, a trial-ending reminder was shown, the upgrade page or the trial-end offer was shown, a plan was chosen. It also reports, at most once a day, that the speed was changed that day, so we can tell how many installs are actually used. When you remove the extension, Chrome opens our uninstall page, and that page reports one removal together with the extension version, the stage the install was in (for example, during the free trial or after a purchase) and roughly how long ago it was installed (the same day, the next day, up to a week, up to a month, longer). Each report carries only the name of the step and the extension version — no install ID, no settings, nothing about the pages you visit. The server uses your IP address only to determine your country, does not store it, and keeps only daily per-country totals of each step.

If you buy All Speeds

Payment happens on a checkout page run by Stripe for our payment provider EasyPay. Your card details are handled by Stripe and never reach the extension or our server. After the payment we receive: the install ID, the plan you bought, the subscription status and paid period, the payment ID and your email address. We use the email only to restore your purchase in another browser and to answer your support requests. Stripe’s privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy.

What we never do

We do not sell or share your data for advertising, we do not show ads, and we do not use your data for anything other than providing the extension and the upgrade. We disclose data to public authorities only when the law requires us to.

Legal basis (EEA and UK)

The license check and the purchase data are processed to perform our contract with you. The anonymous step counts and determining your country from the IP address are based on our legitimate interest in running and improving the extension. Your email is processed to perform the contract and, when you write to us, to answer your request.

Where data is processed

Our license server is hosted by Vercel; payments are processed by Stripe and EasyPay. These providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States, under their own data protection terms. All requests from the extension to our server use HTTPS.

Our web pages

The welcome, thank-you and uninstall pages on kimdav178.github.io use Umami, a cookie-free analytics service, to count visits. The uninstall page embeds a Google Form that you may fill in voluntarily.

How long we keep data

Purchase records are kept while the purchase is active and for as long as needed to handle refunds, disputes and accounting. You can ask us to delete your email at any time; the purchase then stays tied to the install ID only.

Your rights

You can ask what we store about your install or purchase, ask for a correction or deletion, or object to processing based on our legitimate interest. Write to kimdav178@gmail.com or use the contact form: forms.gle/mNnwQanA5D8eQSXXA. Include your install ID (shown on the extension’s upgrade page) or the email from your receipt. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has made a purchase, contact us and we will delete the related data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
